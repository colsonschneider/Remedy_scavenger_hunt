The Great Remedy Hunt

Thirty clues hidden across Kensington and the Bow River pathways. Solve each puzzle to discover a location — then take a team photo there. First team back to the office with all 30 wins.

Choose your team

Each team has its own QR code. Tap your team to get started.

How it works

  1. Tap your team above (or scan your team's QR code).
  2. You'll land on your team page with all 30 clue cards.
  3. Open any clue, solve the puzzle to reveal a Calgary location.
  4. Walk to the spot, take a team photo, and upload it on the clue page.
  5. Tap "I solved it" to mark the clue complete.
  6. Solve all 30 and head back to the office for the prize.

Progress is saved on your device for your team — your team can keep tabs from any phone using the same QR code.
