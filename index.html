How it works

Quick refresher — beat the other teams to all 17 clues.

  1. Tap a clue card to open the puzzle.
  2. Solve the puzzle — it reveals a Calgary location.
  3. Walk there and take a team photo at the spot.
  4. Come back to the clue page and tap "I solved it ✓".
  5. Your whole team sees the solve within seconds.

Tips

  • Clues can be solved in any order.
  • Stuck? Skip it and come back later.
  • Save clue #17 for the finale.
  • The Switch button in the corner changes teams.
Heads up: Your team's progress is shared in real time across every teammate's phone.
