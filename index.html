How it works

  1. Tap your team and enter the password your host gave you.
  2. You'll land on your team's clue board.
  3. Open any clue, solve the puzzle to reveal a Calgary spot.
  4. Walk to the spot and tap "I solved it".
  5. The whole team sees every solve in real time.
